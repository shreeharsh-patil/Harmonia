## 1. From Search to Sung Line *(Recommended)*
- **Device & Form**: The feature as the hook + object-to-object chain (each beat exits through the real UI control that opens the next world).
- **Viewer Sees & Feels**: Opens immediately on Harmonia's real dark-emerald Home screen (`Barsaat`, `Arz Kiya Hai`, `cosy coding sessions`) scrolling cleanly inside the full-bleed 9:16 mobile canvas, with crisp kinetic headlines and zero fake phone-inside-phone borders. - **Beats**: `hook (split race) → tension (conventional stream stalls) → reveal (collapse into Harmonia player) → proof (lyrics + offline downloads) → end card`.
